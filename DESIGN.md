# Fincards Design System

## Brand Identity
**Brand Name:** Fincards
**Product:** Premium Digital Wedding Invitations
**Theme:** Warm Clean, Elegant, Modern, and Welcoming.

## Color Palette
### Primary Colors
- **Cream/Off-White (Backgrounds):** `#FDF6EC`
- **Dark Brown (Text/Headings):** `#2A2118`
- **Terracotta/Orange (Accents/Buttons):** `#E8553E`

### Secondary Colors
- **Warm Beige:** `#F7EBD7`
- **Muted Brown:** `#5C4A3A`
- **Mustard Yellow:** `#F5C842`
- **Soft Peach:** `#F4A38F`
- **Deep Rust:** `#C43F2A`

## Typography
- **Headings & Titles:** `Montserrat` (Bold, Elegant, Clear)
- **Body Text & Labels:** `Biryani` (Regular, Clean, Highly Readable)
- **Script Accents (Decorative):** `Tangerine`
- **Hero Couple Names (Elegant Script):** `Great Vibes`

## Design Guidelines for Instagram Ads
- **Dimensions:** 1080x1080 (Square) or 1080x1350 (Portrait).
- **Layout:** Minimalist. Give plenty of breathing room (white space/negative space) around text and images.
- **Backgrounds:** Use the `#FDF6EC` cream background or soft gradient transitions using secondary colors. Avoid harsh or pure white backgrounds.
- **Imagery:** Focus on romantic, warm, and clean imagery. When showing product mockups (phones/laptops), use soft drop shadows to make them pop elegantly against the warm background.
- **Logos:** Ensure the Fincards logo (rose gold/copper on cream) is placed subtly but visibly in one of the corners (e.g., top right or bottom center) on every post to build brand recall.
- **Text Alignment:** Left-aligned or centered, keeping it highly readable. Use `Montserrat` for the main hook/headline in `#2A2118` or `#E8553E`.

## Voice and Tone (Visual)
The visuals should evoke feelings of warmth, luxury, romance, and trust. Avoid cluttered elements. Everything should feel meticulously placed and premium.
